# Product vision

Gravity Force is an independent platform for investigating and improving a GeForce NOW session. The website, Chrome extension, and iOS experience are the three product tracks.

The product is not a website that merely claims to make GeForce NOW faster. It measures what is happening, identifies what can be improved, applies changes that are technically possible, and tests whether those changes actually help.

## Working name

Gravity Force. Chromerty is not used. Other candidates (Nova Force, Velocity Cloud, Gravity Boost, CloudStrike, Flux Gaming, Phantom Stream, Hyperstream) remain creative options, not cleared trademarks.

The name can change later without rebuilding the application.

Do not present Gravity Force as an official NVIDIA product or imply NVIDIA endorsement.

## First network feature

Bandwidth Investigation Mode is the first network-related feature, not a later optimization trick.

It must:

1. Record the bandwidth GeForce NOW reports as available.
2. Record the bandwidth it reports as being used.
3. Calculate utilization percentage.
4. Record resolution, frame rate, and streaming-quality settings where available.
5. Compare measurements across sessions.
6. Help determine whether low utilization is normal for the selected quality or associated with a performance problem.
7. Provide troubleshooting recommendations based on the evidence.

If the extension cannot read those statistics directly, users enter the diagnostic values or follow a guided workflow.
